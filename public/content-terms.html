Content Terms for ViaHimalaya

Version: 2026-10-29 · Last Updated: October 29, 2026

These terms apply when you add a place to a trek in the ViaHimalaya app (the "App"): a photo, an optional note and a position. We call what you add "Your Content". You accept these terms by ticking the box when you save a place. They are short, so please read them.

In short

  • What you add stays yours.
  • You let us store it and show it to other users of ViaHimalaya.
  • Only add photos you took yourself, or have permission to share.
  • We look at every place before anyone else can see it, and we can remove it later. So can you, by asking us.

1. Your Content stays yours

You keep the copyright in your photos and in the words of your notes. These terms do not transfer ownership to us. They give us the permission described in Section 2, and nothing more.

2. What you allow us to do

When you save a place, you give ViaHimalaya a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to:

  • store and copy Your Content on our servers and on those of the companies that host them for us (see our Privacy Policy);
  • resize, compress and crop your photo so that it displays well on a phone;
  • show Your Content to other users, in the App and on viahimalaya.com, once we have approved it; and
  • use the position and the note to improve our trek information, for example to add a campsite or a source of water to a trek.

We do not pay you for this, and we will not sell Your Content. The licence lasts until Your Content is removed, whether by you or by us. Copies held in backups or caches can take a little longer to disappear.

3. What you promise

  • You took the photo yourself, or the person who did has given you permission to share it.
  • The place is real, and the position is where you say it is, as far as you know.
  • Your Content does not break the law or anyone else's rights.
  • You are old enough to agree to these terms: the App is not for children under 13 (16 in the European Union).

4. What not to add

  • Photos you did not take, including pictures copied from websites, other apps or other maps.
  • Photos in which a person can be recognised, unless they are happy for it to be shown. Please never add photos of children.
  • Nudity, violence, hateful or abusive content, or anything illegal.
  • Phone numbers, addresses or other personal details of anyone.
  • Places or notes you know to be wrong or misleading, advertising, or spam.

5. Review and removal

A place is private until we have looked at it. We may approve, reject or remove any place at any time, without giving a reason. If you break these terms, we may stop you from adding places.

You can ask us to remove a place you added at any time by emailing us (see Section 8). We will remove it promptly, and in any case within 30 days.

6. Reporting a problem

If you think something shown in ViaHimalaya is inappropriate, or uses your photo or your words without your permission, email us with the details. We will look at it promptly and remove anything that breaks these terms.

7. Accuracy and safety

Places and notes come from other users and may be wrong or out of date. The mountains are not forgiving: use your own judgement on the trail, and do not rely on a place or a note for your safety.

If we change these terms in a way that matters, the App will ask you to agree again the next time you add a place. The version at the top of this page is the one the App records when you save a place.
